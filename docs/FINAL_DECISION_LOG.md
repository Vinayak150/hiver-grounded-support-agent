# Final decision log

This submission view curates 15 non-obvious decisions. The complete historical log
remains in [`DECISION_LOG.md`](../DECISION_LOG.md); no historical ADR was deleted.

| # | Decision | Alternatives considered | Why chosen | Trade-off |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Implement independently and cite the two reference repositories only as high-level architectural influences. | Fork either repository; copy its evaluation structures. | Keeps authorship and evidence provenance auditable. | More implementation time and fewer inherited features. |
| 2 | Replace the initial `AmazonHelp` choice with `SpotifyCares` under a volume-saturation rule. | Maximize raw volume; retain the lexicographic Phase 1 winner. | SpotifyCares cleared the 10,000-thread sufficiency threshold and had materially better public-containment and duplication characteristics across every declared sensitivity check. | Gives up extra volume after the sample is already large enough. |
| 3 | Profile the 2.8M-row CSV through an ephemeral SQLite index. | Load the CSV into memory; use distributed infrastructure. | Deterministic joins and reconstruction work on ordinary hardware without committing raw data. | Full reconstruction is slower than an in-memory run on a large machine. |
| 4 | Reconstruct conversations from canonical parent links and protect whole threads. | Use tweet rows independently; trust plural child-link fields. | Parent linkage gives a single lineage and prevents conversational context leaking across partitions. | Ambiguous or malformed lineage must be diagnosed and excluded. |
| 5 | Use chronological TRAIN/DEVELOPMENT/GOLDEN_CANDIDATE partitions. | Deterministic random splitting. | Better approximates deployment drift while keeping model choices confined to earlier data. | Class and topic prevalence can shift across time. |
| 6 | Iteratively remove exact and high-similarity cross-split collisions from later partitions. | Keep all volume; check exact duplicates only. | Independent leakage checks reached zero declared cross-split collisions. | Removes data and only protects against the published similarity rules. |
| 7 | Treat the nine-intent classifier targets as TRAIN-derived weak groups, not human truth. | Claim unsupervised clusters as labels; wait to build any classifier. | Enables an interpretable pipeline while accurately bounding what its accuracy can mean. | Final intent accuracy remains unknowable until human gold exists. |
| 8 | Freeze a 200-case final set before agent development, with 160 representative and 40 challenge cases. | Evaluate on DEVELOPMENT; choose final cases after viewing errors. | Prevents result-driven test selection while preserving deliberate stress coverage. | Challenge enrichment means the benchmark is not a natural prevalence sample. |
| 9 | Store AI provisional suggestions separately from human annotations. | Pre-fill them as accepted labels; discard review assistance entirely. | Supports later review without laundering model output into gold. | Human confirmation is still required for every accepted row. |
| 10 | Include an always-escalate fixed baseline and an interpretable lexical-neighbor baseline. | Compare only against the proposed agent; use an embedding/LLM baseline. | Exposes safety-through-abstention and retrieval value with deterministic, credential-free systems. | Neither baseline represents a strong production system. |
| 11 | Calibrate evidence and automation thresholds only from unlabeled DEVELOPMENT distributions. | Tune on judge pass rates; use frozen data; choose arbitrary constants. | Makes the pre-gold heuristic reproducible without optimizing on the final set. | Distribution quantiles are not correctness- or safety-optimal thresholds. |
| 12 | Default to deterministic extractive generation plus conservative risk, evidence, and verifier gates. | Unrestricted generation; always automate; always escalate. | Prevents silent external facts and makes every automation decision traceable. | Extractive replies can be irrelevant, generic, or incomplete even when grounded. |
| 13 | Treat the Groq LLM judge as a DEVELOPMENT diagnostic rather than truth. | Publish its pass rate as the final score; omit quality assessment. | Repeatability and order checks exposed material instability, including a 37.5% order-flip rate at N=8. | Useful qualitative signals cannot support a validated headline claim yet. |
| 14 | Reject the bounded Phase 4.1 remediation and retain original Phase 4. | Promote the newer version; continue tuning against judge scores. | Across 45 complete pairs, quality deltas were negative and critical failures increased from three to five. | The known original-agent defects remain for future human-guided work. |
| 15 | Fail closed on final evaluation until 150–250 genuine finalized human labels exist. | Use provisional labels; impute missing labels; report no benchmark gate. | Protects the only legitimate final benchmark from circular or fabricated evidence. | The repository can be engineering-ready while the headline result remains pending human work. |
